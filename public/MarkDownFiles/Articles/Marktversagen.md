Markt-failure leads to Market success.
Economics is everywhere, our world functions by its laws. It dictates how markets function, why markets or governments fail, and even how you, the consumer, behave. This article will focus on one key real-life example: Manchester United's proposed plan to renovate the old Trafford stadium.

Market failure is a situation where people not involved in making/selling or using a product are affected. The effect can be either positive or negative and these people will be called third party/parties. By this definition, most markets are in failure. Market failure can either be fixed by government intervention or by other policies.

In the case of Old Trafford, to understand the benefits of this decision. We must first look at the problem, existing market failure, and then the proposed solution and its predicted benefits.

The current situation is that the stadium is old and lacks good equipment. The surrounding areas are also very underdeveloped, especially the public infrastructure. Old Trafford lacks modern public transport which is causing many problems for the people. This has led to major levels of congestion and also sound pollution.

This regeneration of the stadium will fix all these problems as the demand to watch football is very high and because of this high demand, many people will come to watch it. This increased consumption will fuel the fire and motivate the government and other investors to start investing and developing the area. This is exactly what Manchester United proposed in the plan.

They wish to achieve this outcome by doing a public-private partnership with the government where the football team will fund the renovation, and the government will fund the infrastructure development in the area.

This partnership will have many long-term effects. 92,000 jobs will be created (increasing the employment rate and allowing many people to live a better life), 17,000 new houses, fixing the growing housing problem, and of course, 1.8 million additional visitors per year. These additional visitors will have a huge positive effect on the local business as now more people will be using their services or products. This type of effect is called a positive externality of production where the third parties are benefiting positively by the choices of people involved in the market.
